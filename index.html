FAQ

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch. The AI runs on your own device (your phone or computer does the work), so it costs us nothing to run — and we pass that on. No signup, no credits, no watermarks.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

Never. The entire process happens locally in your browser using WebAssembly. Your images never leave your device, which also makes it faster than cloud tools.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG and WebP up to 25MB. Output is always a transparent PNG at your image's original resolution.

Why does the first run take longer?

Your browser downloads the AI model once (~40MB). After that it's cached, and removals take just a few seconds.

Can I use the results commercially?

Yes — the output is yours. Use it in products, ads, thumbnails and client work with no attribution required.
